NEON CORE

Roll a light core down a neon track. Grab every data crystal, dodge the red hurdles, clear the sector.

PLAY

Free in your browser. Works with a keyboard or a touchscreen.

Inside a sector

How to play

Controls

WASD or the arrow keys to move
Space to jump over gaps
joystick on touchscreens
jump button on touchscreens

Rules

  • Collect every cyan data crystal to clear the sector.
  • Red hurdles cost a shield, or a life if you have no shield left.
  • You get 2 free shields every day and 3 lives per run.
  • A green box makes your core smaller and faster.
  • An orange box makes it heavier, so it jumps higher.
  • Clear a sector quickly to earn three stars.

Privacy Policy

Neon Core: the Android app (com.akshat009.neoncore) and the web version at play.asaxenadev.com.
Effective date: 17 November 2026

In short: Neon Core has no accounts and does not send your game progress to us. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob (in the app) and from ad partners on the web; those ad providers collect some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Who we are

Neon Core is made by Akshat Saxena, an independent developer in Lucknow, India. Questions about this policy: see Contact.

Data the game keeps on your device

To save your progress, the game stores the following only on your device (in the app's storage or your browser's local storage):

  • your Data balance, cleared sectors, stars and best times, and the run you can continue;
  • cores you bought or stored, shields, and shop boosts;
  • settings such as music, sound, vibration, graphics and stick sensitivity, and which tutorial tips you have seen.

We do not receive this information. On Android it may be included in your phone's own backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), which Google manages under its own terms.

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any account details. The game has no sign-in, no chat and no analytics or tracking service of its own.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads:

  • Android app: Google AdMob shows rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one), full-screen ads between some sectors, and a banner on the Run Reset screen.
  • Web version: ads may come from Google (AdSense for H5 games) or AppLixir.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these providers may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. They may use cookies or similar technologies on the web.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions the Android app uses

  • Internet / network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Vibrate: for short vibrations during play (you can turn them off in Settings).

Children

Neon Core is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time: on Android, uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Neon Core → Storage → Clear storage); on the web, clear this site's data in your browser. For data held by ad providers, use the controls linked in the Advertising section.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
